Autumn Concert 2026

Saturday 17 October 2026

7:30pm

St John’s Waterloo, Waterloo Road, London SE1 8TY

Book tickets

We open our 2026–27 season with a programme that ranges from the bustle of Edwardian London to the darker corners of early twentieth-century Budapest.

Elgar's Cockaigne (In London Town) is a vivid, warm-hearted concert overture evoking the sounds and energy of the capital.

Mozart's Piano Concerto No. 21, one of his best-loved works for the instrument, features soloist Milda Daunoraite.

The concert concludes with the complete version of Bartók's The Miraculous Mandarin, a visceral and dramatic score written for a ballet pantomime that scandalised audiences at its 1926 premiere.

Programme

  • Elgar — Cockaigne (In London Town)
  • Mozart — Piano Concerto No. 21. Milda Daunoraite piano
  • Bartók — The Miraculous Mandarin (complete)
