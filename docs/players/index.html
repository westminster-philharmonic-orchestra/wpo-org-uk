Join the Orchestra

The Westminster Philharmonic Orchestra welcomes experienced amateur musicians who share a passion for ambitious orchestral repertoire. We are actively seeking string players, although we’d love to hear from other instruments.

Rehearsals

Rehearsals are held on Sundays, 14:30–17:30 at:

King Solomon Academy, Penfold Street, London NW1 6RX  (map)  (TFL)

Please bring a music stand.

Concerts

Dress code for indoor concerts: Women, long black; Men, black shirt and trousers, no tie, jacket optional.

Membership

As a community orchestra, we need our members’ support to allow us to put on our ambitious programmes.

The subscription is £160 for the full season payable before the first concert, with a discount of £20 (total cost £140) if paid by the second rehearsal. The subscription for an individual concert is £40, due by the day before the event. For concessions, the annual fee is £80 or £20 per concert. Anyone having a problem with the fees should contact the membership secretary.
